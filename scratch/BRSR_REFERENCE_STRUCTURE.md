# Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) Reference Structure
**Regulatory Framework**: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CMD-2/P/CIR/2021/562
**Reference Format**: Standard Statutory Form BRSR (FY 2025-26 Alignment)

---

## 1. Overview & General Nomenclature
* **Report Title**: Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR)
* **Reporting Boundary**: Standalone vs. Consolidated reporting
* **Applicable Financial Year**: e.g., FY 2025-26 (April 1, 2025 – March 31, 2026)
* **Reporting Entity**: Listed / Mandated Corporate Entity

---

## 2. Section A: General Disclosures

### I. Details of the Listed Entity
1. **Corporate Identity Number (CIN)** of the Listed Entity
2. **Name of the Listed Entity**
3. **Year of Incorporation**
4. **Registered Office Address**
5. **Corporate Address**
6. **E-mail ID**
7. **Telephone Number**
8. **Website URL**
9. **Financial Year** for which reporting is being done
10. **Name of the Stock Exchange(s)** where shares are listed
11. **Paid-up Capital** (INR / Base Currency)
12. **Name and contact details** of the person who may be contacted in case of queries on the BRSR report (Name, Designation, Email, Phone)
13. **Reporting Boundary**: Disclosures made on a Standalone basis or Consolidated basis

### II. Products / Services
14. **Details of business activities** (accounting for 90% of the turnover):
    - Description of Main Activity
    - Description of Business Activity
    - % of Turnover of the entity
15. **Products/Services sold by the entity** (accounting for 90% of the entity’s Turnover):
    - Product / Service
    - NIC Code (5-digit National Industrial Classification)
    - % of total Turnover contributed

### III. Operations
16. **Number of locations where plants and/or operations/offices of the entity are situated**:
    - National Locations: Number of Plants, Number of Offices, Total
    - International Locations: Number of Plants, Number of Offices, Total
17. **Markets served by the entity**:
    - a. Number of locations (National / International state/country count)
    - b. What is the contribution of exports as a percentage of the total turnover of the entity?
    - c. A brief on types of customers

### IV. Employees & Workers
18. **Details as at the end of Financial Year**:
    - **a. Employees and workers** (Total, Male (No. & %), Female (No. & %)):
      - Permanent Employees (A)
      - Other than Permanent Employees (B)
      - Total Employees (A + B)
      - Permanent Workers (C)
      - Other than Permanent Workers (D)
      - Total Workers (C + D)
    - **b. Differently abled Employees and workers**:
      - Permanent Differently Abled Employees
      - Permanent Differently Abled Workers
19. **Participation / Inclusion / Representation of Women**:
    - Board of Directors (Total, No. of female directors, % of female directors)
    - Key Management Personnel (KMP) (Total, No. of female KMP, % of female KMP)
20. **Turnover rate for permanent employees and workers** (Past 3 years trend: FY, FY-1, FY-2 for Male, Female, Total)

### V. Holding, Subsidiary & Associate Companies
21. **Names of holding / subsidiary / associate companies / joint ventures**:
    - Name of company
    - Indicate whether Holding/ Subsidiary/ Associate/ Joint Venture
    - % shares held by listed entity
    - Does the entity participate in the Business Responsibility initiatives of the listed entity? (Yes/No)

### VI. CSR Details
22. **Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)**:
    - (i) Whether CSR is applicable as per section 135 of Companies Act, 2013 (Yes/No)
    - (ii) Turnover (in INR)
    - (iii) Net worth (in INR)

### VII. Transparency & Disclosures Compliances
23. **Complaints / Grievances on any of the principles (Principles 1 to 9)** under the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC):
    - Stakeholder group (Communities, Investors, Shareholders, Employees/workers, Customers, Value Chain Partners, Others)
    - Grievance Redressal Mechanism in place (Yes/No)
    - Number of complaints filed during the year
    - Number of complaints pending resolution at close of the year
24. **Overview of the entity's material responsible business conduct issues**:
    - Material Issue Identified
    - Indicate whether Risk or Opportunity (R/O)
    - Rationale for identifying the risk / opportunity
    - In case of risk, approach to adapt or mitigate
    - Financial implications of the risk or opportunity (Positive / Negative)

## 3. Section B: Management and Process Disclosures

### Policy and Management Processes
1. **Policy Coverage**: Whether the entity's policy/policies cover each of the 9 NGRBC principles (Yes/No for P1 through P9).
2. **Board Approval**: Has the policy been approved by the Board? (Yes/No)
3. **Web Link**: Web Link of the Policies, if available.
4. **Translation into Procedures**: Whether the entity has translated the policy into procedures. (Yes/No)
5. **Value Chain Coverage**: Do the policies extend to the value chain partners and suppliers? (Yes/No)
6. **National / International Standards**: Name of national and international codes/certifications/labels/standards adopted (e.g., ISO 14001, ISO 50001, OHSAS 18001, SA 8000, GRI, CDP, GLEC Framework).
7. **Specific Commitments, Goals and Targets**: Specific commitments, goals and targets set by the entity with defined timelines.
8. **Performance against Targets**: Performance of the entity against the specific commitments, goals and targets along with reasons in case the same are not met.

### Governance, Leadership and Oversight
9. **Statement by Director responsible for the business responsibility report**, highlighting ESG challenges, targets and achievements.
10. **Details of the highest authority responsible for implementation and oversight of the BRSR policy/policies**:
    - Name, Designation, DIN
11. **Details of Review of NGRBCs by the Company**:
    - Performance against policies and follow up action
    - Compliance with statutory requirements of relevance to the principles, and rectification of any non-compliances
12. **Independent Third-Party Assurance**:
    - Has the entity carried out independent assessment/ evaluation of the working of its policies by an external agency? (Yes/No)
    - If yes, provide name of the external agency and assurance standard (Limited / Reasonable).
    *NOTE: Default in CarbonIQ is strictly "Not Provided" unless explicitly confirmed by the enterprise auditor.*

## 4. Section C: Principle-Wise Performance Disclosures

### Principle 1: Integrity, Ethics, Transparency & Accountability
* Essential Indicator 1: Coverage of training and awareness programmes on NGRBC principles.
* Essential Indicator 2: Details of fines / penalties / action taken by regulatory authorities for anti-competitive conduct or corruption.
* Essential Indicator 3: Details of anti-corruption or anti-bribery policy and grievance redressal mechanisms.

### Principle 2: Safe & Sustainable Goods & Services
* Essential Indicator 1: Percentage of R&D and capital expenditure (capex) investments in technologies to improve environmental and social impacts.
* Essential Indicator 2: Sustainable sourcing procedures (Percentage of inputs sourced sustainably).
* Essential Indicator 3: Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) plan and implementation details.
* Essential Indicator 4: Life Cycle Assessments (LCA) conducted for products/services (Yes/No).
* Essential Indicator 5: Percentage of recycled or reused input materials used to manufacture products.

### Principle 3: Employee Well-being
* Essential Indicator 1: Measures for well-being of employees & workers (Health insurance, Accident insurance, Maternity benefits, Paternity benefits, Day care facilities).
* Essential Indicator 2: Details of retirement benefits (PF, Gratuity, ESI).
* Essential Indicator 3: Accessibility of workplaces for differently abled employees and workers.
* Essential Indicator 4: Equal opportunity policy details.
* Essential Indicator 5: Return to work and retention rates of permanent employees and workers that took parental leave.
* Essential Indicator 6: Occupational health and safety management system (OHSMS) coverage.

### Principle 4: Stakeholder Engagement
* Essential Indicator 1: Processes for identifying key stakeholder groups.
* Essential Indicator 2: Channels of communication, frequency of engagement, and purpose of consultation with key stakeholder groups (including vulnerable and marginalized groups).

### Principle 5: Human Rights
* Essential Indicator 1: Training on human rights issues and policies.
* Essential Indicator 2: Minimum wage payment compliance details (% of employees and workers paid equal to or more than minimum wage).
* Essential Indicator 3: Mechanism to prevent child labor, forced/involuntary labor, and sexual harassment.

### Principle 6: Environmental Protection (⭐ CORE CARBONIQ ENGINE INTEGRATION)
* **Essential Indicator 1: Energy Consumption**:
  - Total electricity consumption (A) in Joules / kWh / MWh (Renewable & Non-Renewable)
  - Total fuel consumption (B) in Joules / Liters / GJ (Stationary Diesel, Natural Gas, Fleet Fuel)
  - Energy consumption through other sources (C)
  - **Total Energy Consumption (A + B + C)**
  - **Energy Intensity** per rupee of turnover or per physical unit of output
* **Essential Indicator 2: Water Withdrawal & Consumption**:
  - Surface water, Groundwater, Third party water, Seawater / desalinated water, Others
  - Total volume of water consumption
  - Water Intensity per rupee of turnover / physical output
* **Essential Indicator 3: Air Emissions (Non-GHG)**:
  - NOx, SOx, Particulate Matter (PM), Persistent Organic Pollutants (POP), Volatile Organic Compounds (VOC)
* **Essential Indicator 4: Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Emissions (Scope 1 & Scope 2)** (⚡ Direct CarbonIQ Database Mapping):
  - **Total Scope 1 Emissions** (Metric tonnes of CO₂ equivalent / t CO₂e):
    - Stationary Combustion
    - Mobile Combustion
    - Fugitive Emissions & Process Emissions
  - **Total Scope 2 Emissions** (Metric tonnes of CO₂ equivalent / t CO₂e):
    - Location-Based Scope 2
    - Market-Based Scope 2 (Accounting for contractual instruments / Green Tariffs / RECs)
  - **Total Scope 1 and Scope 2 Emissions (t CO₂e)**
  - **GHG Emissions Intensity** per rupee of turnover or per physical unit of product/shipment
* **Essential Indicator 5: Scope 3 GHG Emissions** (⚡ Direct CarbonIQ GLEC/GHG Mapping):
  - Upstream Transportation & Distribution (tonne-km freight)
  - Downstream Transportation & Distribution
  - Purchased Goods & Services (Cradle-to-gate material emissions, Steel, etc.)
  - Business Travel & Employee Commute
  - **Total Scope 3 Emissions (t CO₂e)**
  - Scope 3 Emission Intensity
* **Essential Indicator 6: Green Initiatives & Emission Reduction Projects**:
  - Specific initiatives undertaken to reduce GHG emissions and waste, with reduction achievements.
* **Essential Indicator 7: Waste Management & Circular Economy**:
  - Total waste generated (Plastic, E-waste, Hazardous, Bio-medical, Non-hazardous)
  - Waste diverted from disposal (Recycled, Re-used, Recovered)
  - Waste directed to disposal (Incineration, Landfill)
* **Essential Indicator 8: Ecological Sensitive Areas**:
  - Operations in or near ecologically sensitive areas (National parks, wildlife sanctuaries, etc.)

### Principle 7: Responsible Public & Regulatory Policy
* Essential Indicator 1: Affiliations with trade and industry associations.
* Essential Indicator 2: Details of corrective action taken or underway on any issues related to anti-competitive conduct.

### Principle 8: Inclusive Growth & Equitable Development
* Essential Indicator 1: Details of Social Impact Assessments (SIA) of projects undertaken by the entity.
* Essential Indicator 2: Details of project(s) for which ongoing rehabilitation and resettlement (R&R) is underway.
* Essential Indicator 3: Mechanism to receive and redress grievances of the community.
* Essential Indicator 4: Percentage of input material sourced directly from MSMEs/small producers.

### Principle 9: Consumer Value & Responsibility
* Essential Indicator 1: Mechanisms for consumer feedback and grievances.
* Essential Indicator 2: Turnover of products and/services as a percentage of turnover from state/central regulated products.
* Essential Indicator 3: Number of consumer complaints in respect of data privacy, advertising, cyber security, delivery of essential services.
* Essential Indicator 4: Details of instances of product recalls on account of safety issues.
